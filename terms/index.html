Subscription Terms

Effective October 8, 2026

What you buy

Liftora Pro is an auto-renewable subscription for your Apple ID. It unlocks AI Coach, Body Scan, AI Transformation, weekly AI reviews, and advanced progress.

Length and price

Liftora Pro Monthly lasts 1 month and costs 9.99 USD. Liftora Pro Yearly lasts 1 year and costs 39.99 USD. Apple charges the price shown on the subscription screen when you confirm the purchase. The price can differ by country and currency.

Auto-renewal

Each plan renews automatically for the same length. Apple charges your Apple ID when you confirm, and again within 24 hours before the current period ends, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before then.

How to cancel

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Liftora Pro, then Cancel. You can also use Manage Subscriptions. Access continues until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the app or deleting data inside Liftora does not cancel the subscription.

Restore

Restore Purchases on the subscription screen or in Settings reloads a plan already purchased with this Apple ID.

Price changes

If the price changes, Apple asks you to agree before charging the new price.

Subscription Terms

Effective October 9, 2026

What you buy

ProFit/AI Pro unlocks AI Coach, Body Scan, AI Transformation, weekly AI reviews, and advanced progress. Monthly and yearly are auto-renewable subscriptions. Lifetime is a one-time purchase.

Length and price

ProFit/AI Pro Monthly lasts 1 month and costs 7.99 USD. ProFit/AI Pro Yearly lasts 1 year and costs 39.99 USD. ProFit/AI Pro Lifetime costs 119.99 USD once and does not renew. Apple charges the price shown on the purchase screen when you confirm. The price can differ by country and currency.

Auto-renewal

A monthly or yearly plan renews automatically for the same length. Apple charges your Apple ID when you confirm, and again within 24 hours before the current period ends, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before then. Lifetime does not renew.

How to cancel

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then ProFit/AI Pro, then Cancel. You can also use Manage Subscriptions. Access continues until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the app or deleting data inside ProFit/AI does not cancel the subscription.

Restore

Restore Purchases on the subscription screen or in Settings reloads a plan already purchased with this Apple ID.

Price changes

If the price changes, Apple asks you to agree before charging the new price.

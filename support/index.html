Support

Effective October 8, 2026

Contact

For help with the Liftora iPhone app, open a support request. Include your device, iOS version, and what happened. Do not send passwords or payment card numbers.

Subscription not active

If Liftora Pro does not unlock after a purchase, open the subscription screen or Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID that paid.

Cancel renewal

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Liftora Pro, then Cancel. You can also use Manage Subscriptions. Apple handles billing questions for the subscription.

Delete data

Settings includes Delete Account and Data. That erases Liftora data on this iPhone and the private iCloud copy when sync is on. It does not cancel the subscription.

Offline workouts

A workout can be completed without a network connection. It is saved on this iPhone.

Missing Apple features

If Apple Intelligence or Image Playground is unavailable, coaching uses Liftora's built-in answers and a transformation stays a draft.

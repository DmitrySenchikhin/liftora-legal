Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

Data on this iPhone

ProFit/AI stores the profile, workouts, body weight, coach chats, and weekly reviews you create in the app on this iPhone. There is no separate ProFit/AI account server. The profile can include your name, birth date, body measurements, goals, equipment, and health limitations you choose to enter.

Photos

Body Scan and Transformation photos are saved in the app's local files. ProFit/AI does not upload those photos to a ProFit/AI server.

Apple Health

If you allow it, ProFit/AI reads sleep, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, active energy, and steps to estimate readiness. It can write the strength workouts and body weight you log. You can revoke access in the Health app. Deleting your ProFit/AI data removes Health samples that ProFit/AI created.

iCloud

When iCloud is available, the app database can sync in your private iCloud database. Photos stay on this iPhone. When iCloud is not available, your data stays on this iPhone.

On-device intelligence

Apple Intelligence and Image Playground run on the device only when your iPhone supports them. ProFit/AI keeps working when they are unavailable.

Purchases

Apple processes ProFit/AI Pro payments and shows the price before you confirm. ProFit/AI does not receive your card number.

Sharing

ProFit/AI does not sell your information and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Deletion

Delete Account and Data in Settings removes your ProFit/AI profile, workouts, scans, coach chats, and photos from this iPhone and, when sync is on, from your private iCloud database. An App Store subscription stays active until you cancel it with Apple.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open a support request.
